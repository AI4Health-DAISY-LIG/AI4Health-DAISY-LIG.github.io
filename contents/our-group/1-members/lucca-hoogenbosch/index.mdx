### Lucca Hoogenbosch, Msc. Eng. Junior Research Engineer, Grenoble Alpes University 

Lucca Hoogenbosch is an engineer and recent graduate of Grenoble Alpes University, FR, where he completed the “MoSIG” program (Master of Science in Informatics at Grenoble). 
His training focused on applied informatics and modeling with a strong emphasis on algorithms. 
He combines technical rigor with a structured approach to data‑driven decision making, and is interested in roles at the intersection of engineering, analytics, and innovation management.

Currently his work focuses on explainable reinforcement learning applied to biomedical data as well as FAIR, open science, integrated pipelines.
