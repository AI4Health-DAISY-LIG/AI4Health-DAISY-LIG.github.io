### Lookman Jamad, Master 1 Student Methodology and Technology for Health Scholar, University Grenoble Alpes

Lookman Jamad is a Master 1 student in the program Methodology and Technology for Health of the University Grenoble Alpes, where he focuses on applied methodologies in health. 
He worked with us on a FAIR neuroimaging pipeline using voxel-based statistical analysis for the study of functional MRI data. 

Lookman has worked extensively on documenting tool usage in a reproducible manner enabling scientists to access state-of-the-art methodology in voxel-based morphometry.
